Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is there really no minimum order?

Yes. There's no minimum, so you can order from just one box and buy only what your café or event needs.

How long does it take?

Your cups are ready in 10 days after you approve your mockup. Many suppliers quote up to 12 weeks.

Do I need to pay a big deposit or store the cups?

No. There are no setup fees and no big up-front deposits, and you order what you need so there's nothing to store. Many suppliers charge large deposits and storage fees, or leave you to store the stock yourself.

I don't have a design. Can you help?

Yes, and it's free. Send us your logo or just an idea and our team will turn it into a print-ready cup design.

Can I see my cups before they are printed?

Yes. You get a free digital mockup to approve first, and nothing is printed until you say so. If your cups don't match the proof you approved, we reprint them free.

What sizes and cup types do you make?

8oz, 12oz and 16oz cups, in single wall or double wall, printed in full colour.

Are the cups compostable?

Our cups are designed for industrial composting. Read more on our environment page.

Where are the cups made?

Your cups are designed, printed and made in Australia.
