Why local manufacturing

Shorter lead times. Fewer surprises.

Making cups in Sydney instead of importing them changes what you can ask for.

  • Small orders work. Order what you need, when you need it, with no big commitments or excess stock.
  • Fast turnaround. Ready in 10 days, not the six to twelve weeks some suppliers quote.
  • Last-minute reorders. When you run low, your next order isn't on a ship.
  • You approve it first. What you sign off in the mockup is what we print.
  • A real team. Call or email, and a person at Cupco answers.
