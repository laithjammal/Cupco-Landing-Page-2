Questions

Straight answers.

Are your cups compostable?

Our cups are designed for industrial composting. At a commercial composting facility they break down into compost instead of sitting in landfill. They aren't designed for home compost bins.

What are the cups made from?

Sustainably sourced paper with a plant-based lining, used in place of the conventional plastic lining most paper cups have. Your cups arrive in boxes made from recycled paper.

Where should customers put the cups after use?

In a bin collected for industrial composting, where one is available, and not in the standard recycling bin. Take the lid and any sleeve off first. This page covers the cup itself, so ask us about the lids and sleeves you plan to use.

Is the printing safe for food and drink?

We print with Ricoh toner that has been independently assessed for use in food packaging. If you need documentation for your own records, contact us.

Does ordering fewer cups really reduce waste?

Yes. We make cups to order and there's no minimum, so you buy what you'll actually use. That means less excess stock and fewer unused cups thrown out when a design changes or an event is over.

How can my café or event get the cups composted?

It depends on your council, venue or waste provider. Ask your waste contractor about an industrial compost collection. Tell us where you're based and we'll help where we can.

Ready for greener cups?

Send your logo or idea and roughly how many cups. We'll send a free mockup and a price, usually the same day.
