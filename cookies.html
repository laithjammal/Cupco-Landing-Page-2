Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Cupco uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your choices, and tell us how the site is being used.

Your choice

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept non-essential cookies. Until you accept, Google's analytics and advertising cookies are not allowed to store data on your device. You can change your mind at any time by clicking Cookie settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third parties

Google may process data collected through these cookies outside Australia. You can learn more and manage Google's ad settings at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies. To opt out of Google Analytics across all sites, use Google's browser add-on.
